Delivery area

Where we deliver, and when

We deliver ourselves, by cargo bike in the city and by electric van outside it. That keeps the area small, but it means you deal with the same two people every week.

Two zones

  • Zone 1, city of Zürich

    Postcodes 8001 to 8099. Delivery on Tuesday or Thursday, included in every plan.

  • Zone 2, around the city

    8952 Schlieren, 8953 Dietikon, 8152 Opfikon, 8302 Kloten, 8304 Wallisellen, 8600 Dübendorf, 8700 Küsnacht, 8802 Kilchberg, 8134 Adliswil and 8800 Thalwil. Delivery on Wednesday, CHF 40 per month.

  • Somewhere else

    Write to us. If a few offices nearby ask, we add a route.

What a delivery looks like

Beans come in 1 kg bags in a returnable crate. We bring them to your reception or straight to the kitchen, whichever you prefer, and take the empty crate from last week with us.

You recieve a text message the evening before with a two-hour window. If nobody is there, we leave the crate with a neighbour you name, or bring it again the next delivery day.

Delivery days are fixed per office. You can move yours once a quarter.

Not sure about your postcode?

Send us your address and we tell you which zone it is in and which day we would come.
