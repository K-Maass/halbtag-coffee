Partners

The people we work with

Five of us can't do everything. These are the farms, workshops and neighbours who help keep the coffee in your office good, most of them a short ride from the roastery.

Coffee and milk

Our beans

Kaffeegarten Import

Green coffee · Basel, Dreispitz

Kaffeegarten bring in most of our green coffee, from the same cooperatives each year. Mira tastes their samples every spring before we choose the next lots for the house blends.

kaffeegarten-import.ch

Hofmilch Säuliamt

Milk · Affoltern am Albis

A family farm with forty cows. We use their whole milk at our Friday tastings and when we set up a milk system, and several of our offices order from them directly.

hofmilch-saeuliamt.ch

Haferwerk Oerlikon

Oat drink · Zürich Oerlikon

A small team making a barista oat drink from Swiss oats. It foams well in our machines, so it is the one we recommend when an office asks for something without dairy.

haferwerk-oerlikon.ch

Machines and water

About the machines

Kalkfrei Wassertechnik

Water filters · Dietikon

They supply the filter cartridges we fit to every plumbed-in machine and help us test the water hardness in new buildings. More on why it matters in our descaling guide.

kalkfrei-wassertechnik.ch

Brühwerk Ersatzteile

Spare parts · Winterthur

Grinder burrs, seals and brew units for the machines we rent out. When Jonas calls before noon, the part is usually at the workshop the next morning.

bruehwerk-ersatzteile.ch

Seefeld Elektro Service

Electrical safety checks · Zürich Seefeld

Once a year an electrician from Seefeld Elektro checks the wiring and plugs of every machine we rent out. You get a copy of the report.

seefeld-elektroservice.ch

Delivery and what is left over

Where we deliver

Cargoschmiede Binz

Cargo bike workshop · Zürich Binz

They service our two cargo bikes every month and fix them the same day when a chain goes on a Tuesday morning. Without them our city deliveries would stop.

cargoschmiede-binz.ch

Pilzkeller Altstetten

Grounds recycling · Zürich Altstetten

We collect used coffee grounds from offices that want it, and Pilzkeller grow oyster mushrooms on them in an old cellar. What is left after that goes to compost.

pilzkeller-altstetten.ch

Tütenwerk Thalwil

Packaging · Thalwil

They print our 1 kg bags on paper with a compostable lining. The small print run each month means we can change a label when a bean changes.

tuetenwerk-thalwil.ch

Offices and neighbours

Plans for offices

Werkhof Coworking

Coworking · Zürich Hardbrücke

About ninety desks over two floors and one of our first customers. They run two machines from us and host our winter tasting in their event room.

werkhof-coworking.ch

Bürohaus Letten

Shared offices · Zürich Letten

Twelve small firms share one kitchen here. We set it up so each firm gets its own invoice; how much coffee they need we worked out together.

buerohaus-letten.ch

Znünitisch Kreis 4

Charity · Zürich Langstrasse

A volunteer breakfast table for people without a home or a kitchen. We give them the bags left over from each roast week and service their machine for free.

znuenitisch-kreis4.ch
