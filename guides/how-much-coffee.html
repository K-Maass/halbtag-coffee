How much coffee does an office need?

By Mira Keller · Updated 12 August 2026 · 4 min read

Most offices guess, run out on a Thursday and then over-order. The numbers are simpler than they look.

Grams per cup

  • Espresso: 7 to 8 g
  • Lungo or Americano from a bean-to-cup machine: 9 to 10 g
  • Filter coffee: 60 g per litre, about 9 g per mug

Milk drinks use the same coffee as an espresso, so a cappuccino is 7 to 8 g plus the milk.

Cups per person

In the offices we supply, people drink about two cups on a day they are in. With 21 working days a month that comes to around 42 cups, or about 380 g of beans per person. This is the allowance in all our plans.

If many of your team work from home part of the week, the real number is lower. Count the days people are actually there.

Decaf and filter

Plan for about one in ten cups to be decaf. Keep it in a seperate hopper if your machine has two, otherwise people stop asking for it. If you have a batch brewer as well, take its beans out of the total before you split the rest.
