How to descale a bean-to-cup machine

By Jonas Baumann · Updated 3 September 2026 · 6 min read

Limescale builds up inside every coffee machine. It narrows the pipes, makes the heater work harder and, after a while, makes the coffee taste flat. Descaling dissolves it with a mild acid.

If you are on our Team or Office plan, we descale at every service visit and you only need this guide if the machine asks for it in between. On the Filter plan, or with your own machine, this is your job.

How often

Zürich tap water is moderately hard, wich is why most machines here need descaling more often than the manual suggests. As a rough guide:

Cups per dayDescale every
Up to 308 weeks
30 to 804 weeks
More than 802 weeks

With a water filter in the tank you can roughly double these intervals. Change the filter every two months.

What you need

  • Citric acid descaler made for coffee machines. We leave a bottle with every machine we install.
  • A container that holds at least one litre, to put under the spout.
  • About 40 minutes, most of which is waiting.

Do not use vinegar. It is hard to rinse out and it attacks the seals.

Step by step

  1. Empty the drip tray and the grounds container, then put them back.
  2. Take the water filter out of the tank, if there is one.
  3. Fill the tank to the 0.5 l mark with water and add one dose of descaler (100 ml).
  4. Open the menu, choose Care, then Descale. Put the container under the spout and the milk outlet.
  5. The machine pumps the solution through in stages. This takes about 20 minutes. Empty the container when it asks.
  6. Rinse the tank, fill it with fresh water and let the machine run the rinse cycle. Do it twice.
  7. Put the filter back and make one coffee to throw away.

If the machine shows an error during descaling, switch it off, wait a minute and start the program again. If it happens twice, call us on +41 44 000 47 10.
