Getting started

How it works

About the machines

  1. We visit you

    Jonas looks at your kitchen, the water connection and how many people drink coffee. This takes about half an hour and costs nothing.

  2. We install the machine

    Usually within two weeks of signing. We connect it, set the grind for our beans and show two or three people how to clean it.

  3. Beans arrive every week

    Fresh from Monday's roast, in 1 kg bags. If you run low before the next delivery, write to us and we add a bag.

  4. We look after it

    Regular service visits are part of the plan. If something breaks, call us before 10:00 and we come the same day in the city.
