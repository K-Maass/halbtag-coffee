How servicing works

On the Team plan we come every three months, on the Office plan every month. A visit takes about an hour. Jonas descales the machine, cleans the brewing unit and the milk system, checks the grind and replaces seals before they leak.

Before we install anything, we check that your office is in our delivery area, since the people who bring the beans also bring the parts.

When something breaks

Call +41 44 000 47 10 before 10:00 on a working day. In zone 1 we come the same day, in zone 2 the next working day. If a repair takes longer than two days, we bring a replacement machine at no cost.

Repairs and parts are included in the rental. The only exception is damage from using the wrong cleaning products, which we charge at cost.
