# Halbtag Coffee price list

Updated 2026-10-01. This file is the reference for every price on the website.
All prices in CHF, excl. 8.1% VAT.

## Coffee plans

Priced per person per month. Minimum team size: 6 people on every plan.

| Plan   | CHF per person / month | Coffee                          | Delivery           | Service visits                        |
|--------|-----------------------:|---------------------------------|--------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Filter | 15                     | Haus                            | every second week  | twice a year                          |
| Team   | 22                     | Kreis 5 and Decaf               | weekly             | every three months, descaling included |
| Office | 29                     | Haus, Kreis 5 and Decaf         | weekly (twice a week on request) | monthly, milk system cleaning included |

- Every plan includes 380 g of beans per person per month.
- Extra beans: CHF 38 per kg.
- Office plan also includes a one-hour staff session on cleaning and milk.
- Team plan was CHF 19 per person until 2026-09-30. CHF 22 since 2026-10-01.
