Beans

Three coffees, and we keep it that way

An office needs a coffee that tastes the same in March as in November. We change the green coffee when a harvest ends, and taste each new lot until the cup is where it was.

Haus

Brazil, Cerrado Mineiro · Colombia, Huila · filter roast

  • Milk chocolate
  • Hazelnut
  • Low acidity

Our filter coffee. Light enough to taste the Colombian part, round enough that nobody asks for sugar. In the Filter and Office plans.

Kreis 5

Brazil, Sul de Minas · Ethiopia, Sidama · espresso roast

  • Dark chocolate
  • Cherry
  • Brown sugar

The espresso most of our offices drink. It works black and it holds up in a cappuccino. Roasted a little darker than a café would, because office machines extract less.

Decaf

Colombia, Tolima · sugarcane process · espresso roast

  • Caramel
  • Red apple

Decaffeinated with a natural process that uses ethyl acetate from sugarcane. It is definately the coffee people are most surprised by. In the Team and Office plans.

Where the green coffee comes from

We buy from two importers in Switzerland and the Netherlands who publish what they paid the producer. For Kreis 5 we have bought from the same cooperative in Sidama for three harvests now.

We do not sell single-origin lots on subscription. Offices want consistency, and a coffee that changes every month makes that hard. If you want to try something different, ask at a Friday tasting.
