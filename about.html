About us

A roaster and a technician in Kreis 5

We started Halbtag in 2021 because the coffee in most offices we worked in was either from a capsule or from a machine nobody had cleaned in a year.

Why Halbtag

The name comes from how it began. For the first year we both kept our jobs and ran Halbtag in the afternoons, roasting in a shared space in Altstetten and delivering out of a borrowed car.

Since 2023 we have our own roastery in a courtyard on Klingenstrasse, with a 15 kg drum roaster, a small workshop for machine repairs and a table for tastings on Fridays. We supply a little over 60 offices, most of them between 8 and 40 people.

We are still five people, and we want to stay that size. When you call, one of us picks up, and the person who services your machine is the same one who installed it.

The two of us

Mira Keller

Roasting and green coffee

Mira was head roaster at a café roastery in Bern for six years before Halbtag. She sets the roast profiles, buys the green coffee and runs the Friday tastings.

mira@halbtagcoffee.ch

Jonas Baumann

Machines and service

Jonas trained as a service technician for commercial coffee machines and spent nine years repairing them for hotels and canteens. He installs every machine we rent out.

jonas@halbtagcoffee.ch

Besides the two of us, Lea and Samuel do deliveries and packing, and Nora looks after invoices two days a week.

Working with us

We are looking for someone to drive the Wednesday route in zone 2, about 40% to start. You need a category B licence and you should not mind carrying crates. Details are on our jobs page.

Not looking for a job but curious about the roastery? Come to a tasting on a Friday afternoon. Please tell us the day before so we roast enough.
