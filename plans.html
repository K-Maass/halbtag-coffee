Common questions

Who counts as a person?

Everyone who is regularly in the office and drinks coffee. If half your team works from home on Fridays, count them anyway. We adjust the number every quarter if your team grows or shrinks.

What if we drink more than 380 g each?

Then we bring more and charge CHF 38 per kg. Most teams stay within the allowance. Our guide on how much coffee an office needs has the numbers.

Who cleans the machine?

Daily rinsing and emptying the grounds is up to you, the machine tells you when. Descaling is part of every service visit on the Team and Office plans. If you need to do it yourself between visits, our guide walks you through it.

Can we keep our own machine?

On the Filter plan, yes. For espresso we only service the machines we rent out, because we know their parts and can replace them the same day.
